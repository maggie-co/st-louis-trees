Benefit Metrics Explained

Total Stored Carbon (kg)
Cumulative carbon locked in tree biomass. Calculated using allometric equation: Biomass = a(DBH)^b, where DBH is diameter at breast height. Carbon = Biomass × 0.5 (carbon comprises ~50% of dry biomass).
Annual Sequestration (kg/year)
How much carbon the tree removes from atmosphere yearly. Based on annual growth rate and species productivity coefficients. This represents new biomass accumulation minus natural decay.
Stormwater Intercepted (gal/year)
Rainfall captured by leaves and bark before reaching ground. Estimated as: Canopy Area × Leaf Area Index × Annual Rainfall × 0.18 interception factor × 264.172 (conversion to gallons). Reduces urban runoff and flooding.
Pollution Removed (kg/year)
Air pollutants (particulates, ozone, NO₂, PM2.5) filtered by foliage. Based on leaf surface area and species-specific capture efficiency: Pollution = Leaf Area × 0.5 kg/m²/year. Improves air quality.
Energy Savings (kWh/year)
Electricity avoided through shade (cooling) and windbreaks (heating reduction). Formula: (Canopy Area × 0.6 shade factor × 50 kWh/m²/year) ÷ 3 building efficiency factor. Reduces HVAC load.
Survival Probability (%)
Likelihood tree survives to projected year. Calculated using: Survival = (1 - mortality_rate)^age, adjusted for site conditions (health, sentiment, infrastructure conflicts). Lower survival = higher risk investment.
Sources: USDA Forest Service iTree Eco Model • ISO 14067 Carbon Footprinting • NWCC Stormwater Interception Tables • US EPA Air Quality Benefits Database • Nowak et al. (2013)
